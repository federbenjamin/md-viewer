# Privacy

MD Viewer shows Markdown documents on your device.

- **What it stores.** The extension and the app store nothing beyond the current document, and only on your device. The extension keeps the text of recently opened pages in Safari's session storage so the viewer page can show them. That storage is cleared when Safari closes.
- **What it sends.** Nothing leaves your device: there are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.
- **Remote images.** When a document names an image on a website, the viewer fetches that image from that site, as a browser would. The site sees an ordinary image request from your device.
- **Links.** Opening a link goes to that site in your browser, under that site's own privacy policy.
